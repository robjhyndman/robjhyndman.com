The theme this year is "TIME: Timely Insights, Models, and Exploration". As always, the aim is to bring together analysts from academia, industry and government to learn about and discuss open-source tools for business analytics and data science.

## 30 November: Tutorials

Eight half-day tutorials across four tracks, including Nick Tierney on targets, Cynthia Huang on Quarto, Mitchell O'Hara-Wild on exploratory time series analysis, and Hadley Wickham on package development with Positron and AI.

## 1 December: Workshop

I'm giving the keynote, "Spotting the weird ones", on finding anomalies in data using statistical methods and the [`weird`](https://pkg.robjhyndman.com/weird/) R package. That is followed by invited talks on time series, AI workflows and industry perspectives.

The workshop is at 750 Collins Street, Docklands.
